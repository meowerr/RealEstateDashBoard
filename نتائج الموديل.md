Overview: A predictive system was built to estimate apartment market values over a ten year horizon.
Training split: The dataset was divided into 80 percent for training and 20 percent for testing.
Models tested: We benchmarked gradient boosting, random forest, extra trees, lightgbm, and xgboost.
Best model: A super ensemble combining gradient boosting, random forest, and extra trees won.
Accuracy: The model scored an accuracy of 99.46 percent on the investment test set.
Error rate: The mean percentage error remained very low at only 2.13 percent across predictions.
Price calibration: Projections were adjusted so future prices stay realistic and do not shoot too high.
Multipliers: Ten year growth scales moderately between 1.5 and 2.4 times the original value.
Median growth: The median investment apartment moves from 5038000 to 9875000 pounds.
Compounds: Units inside compounds show a higher ten year median multiplier of about 2.07.
Basic units: Standalone urban apartments grow at a steady rate of about 1.72 times base price.
Column update: New ten year estimates were added to all 2431 investment apartments in the data.
Storage: The complete trained model pipeline is saved as an inference file for future valuations.
